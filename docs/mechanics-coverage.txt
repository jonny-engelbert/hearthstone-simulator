Implemented but supported mainly by synthetic/arranged tests
-----------------------------------------------------------
Divine Shield: damage absorption and consumption; no granting card in catalog.
Stealth: selected-target/attack restrictions and loss on attack; no granting card.
Immune: damage prevention and selected-target restrictions; no granting card.
Elusive: spell/hero-power selected-target exclusion; no granting card.
Armor: damage absorption only; no armor-gain card/operation.
Spell Damage scaling: bonuses supplied through test state; no bonus-granting aura card.
Start-of-next-own-turn buff expiration and temporary Health buffs: modified definitions.
Empty/filled permanent mana gains, crystal destruction, Overload unlocking: direct resource tests.
Start/end-of-turn ability hooks and generic AFTER_CARD_PLAY callbacks: modified definitions.
Dynamic Charge/Rush/Windfury state and weapon Windfury: arranged keyword states; general keyword-grant operations are not implemented.
Deep nesting, queued-source invalidation and pathological reaction budgets: synthetic chains plus real-card interactions.

Implemented hooks with no dedicated positive representative-card coverage
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER_ATTACK listener execution: window exists; catalog has no AFTER_ATTACK listener card.
EVENT_TARGET effect recipient: implemented and context-validated; no catalog card uses it and no dedicated positive behavior test was found.
