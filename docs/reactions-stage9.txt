Stage 9: reaction scheduling and interruption windows
====================================================
2026-10-20; foundation-v9 / schema 1.9; 51 definitions.
Named policy: DEPTH_FIRST_REACTIONS_DEATH_WAVES_V1.
Earlier catalog/schema/manifest bytes and prior definition revisions are intact.

Requirements and declared policies
----------------------------------
REA-01 TRIGGER and SECRET abilities contain a typed trigger: event, relation,
subjectKind, optional actorKind and excludeSelf. They cannot ask for a selected
target. TRIGGER is supported on minions/weapons; SECRET is a standalone spell
ability with a matching SECRET mechanic. Weapon Deathrattles are now permitted.
Unknown fields/conditions, incompatible recipients and damage classes fail
validation. Fixed generation/redirect references resolve exact definitions.

REA-02 Supported events: ON_CARD_PLAY, AFTER_CARD_PLAY, BEFORE_SPELL,
AFTER_SPELL, AFTER_SUMMON, DAMAGE, HEAL, MINION_DIED, BEFORE_ATTACK, AFTER_ATTACK.
Relations are ANY, FRIENDLY, ENEMY, SELF. Attack event subjects are defenders;
SELF attack listeners use the attacker instead, including a weapon's own hero.
Event recipients are SELF, EVENT_TARGET, ATTACKER, DEFENDER, CONTROLLER_HERO,
OPPONENT_HERO, in addition to existing selected/query recipients. EVENT_AMOUNT
is legal only in damage/heal listeners and uses the captured event amount.

REA-03 Snapshot matching listeners at event creation, sorted oldest playOrder
first, then definition ability order. New sources cannot join an existing event.
Before delivery, recheck source zone, definition/revision, controller, stamp,
silence and trigger conditions. A bounced/transformed/silenced source loses its
queued reaction. Last-known Deathrattles are the explicit source-presence
exception. Secret and ordinary attack callbacks share chronological ordering.

REA-04 Resolve nested consequences depth-first using an explicit continuation
stack. Reactions raised by one callback effect run before the next effect of
that callback or the next parent listener. No Python call-stack recursion is
used for nested reaction delivery. Damage batches apply all member hits before
any of their damage listeners are delivered. Combat queues defender damage
before attacker return damage, retaining paired-hit simultaneity.

REA-05 Normal death collection remains at outer ability/phase checkpoints.
Acolyte can react to its lethal hit before removal. Remove all minions in a
wave before collecting MINION_DIED listeners. For each corpse, merge its
last-known Deathrattle and surviving death observers in playOrder, resolve
that event, then advance to the next original corpse. Newly caused deaths
wait for the next wave. Existing death-slot placement and aura rules remain.
Cult Master removed in the same wave cannot observe any member of that wave.

REA-06 Card play validates and pays before interruptions. Preserve card-play
counts even when a spell is countered. A spell passes BEFORE_SPELL then
ON_CARD_PLAY, then (if not countered) charges Overload and resolves text or
installs its Secret. Counterspell consumes the paid spell without its text,
Overload or AFTER_SPELL callbacks. AFTER_CARD_PLAY still observes the card play.
Minion On Play callbacks settle before Battlecry, so a source removed there
cannot execute its Battlecry. Summoned minions do not emit a hand-card play.

REA-07 AFTER_CARD_PLAY and AFTER_SPELL use a pre-action listener barrier:
the source must already have existed with the same stamp before that play.
Played minion AFTER_SUMMON is after Battlecry; generated summons snapshot
AFTER_SUMMON as each enters. Multi-summon batches create their members before
delivering pending reactions. Questing/Councilman exclude their own arrival.
This explicit batching policy is not a claim of universal live-client timing.

REA-08 DAMAGE listeners use actual post-immunity/shield damage, including the
portion absorbed by Armor, not capped to remaining Health. Zero damage emits
its existing diagnostic event but does not activate these listeners. HEAL
listeners require actual positive Health restored; no draw for overheal or a
hero heal when the rule requests a minion. Secret damage is SPELL damage and
uses the controller's Spell Damage at trigger time. Pyromancer and weapon
Deathrattle damage remain ABILITY damage and do not receive that modifier.

REA-09 Secrets occupy SECRET, outside the minion board, with unique stamps.
Allow at most five per player and no duplicate card ID, regardless of revision.
Reject before paying. Secrets react only during the opponent's active turn.
Reveal and consume immediately before their callback; an ineligible Secret
stays in place. The current engine state/log remains an omniscient debugger
view; per-player redaction and hidden views remain planned API work.

REA-10 BEFORE_ATTACK stores one mutable proposal. Recheck pending Secrets
against its current defender and actor. Targeted attack Secrets skip actors
that are mortally wounded, pending destroy or no longer in play. Targetless
Explosive Trap can still fire after another Secret neutralizes the attacker.
No-room Noble Sacrifice stays hidden. The defender must remain a living valid
character when an attack Secret activates. Eye for an Eye responds to its
captured DAMAGE event, including lethal damage, before the result check.

REA-11 Counterspell marks its current spell proposal countered. Later counters
in the same queue recheck that flag and remain hidden. Counterspell does not
react to minions, weapons or hero powers. Playing a Secret is itself a spell;
an existing Pyromancer can react after its successful installation.

REA-12 REDIRECT_ATTACK summons one exact Defender definition for the Secret's
controller and changes the proposal's defender. Subsequent queued attack
callbacks use that defender; a hero-only Vaporize/Explosive Trap then skips.
This window mutates and revalidates the existing proposal. It does not rebuild
a second full preparation pass; random Misdirection/forced attacks and multiple
new-proposal passes remain outside this fixture language.

REA-13 After preparation callbacks and deaths, check the result. Cancel combat
if the match ended, an actor left play, or the attacker's arrival stamp changed.
All pre-combat cancellations refund the attack and preserve weapon durability;
do not emulate the supplied obsolete two-death-phase consumption bug. Otherwise
capture both Attack values, consume the attack, remove Stealth, apply paired
hits, drain damage reactions, wear/break the weapon, resolve AFTER_ATTACK,
settle deaths, then check the result. Existing declared targets are not chosen
again against Taunt. Dropping Attack to zero after declaration does not cancel.

REA-14 Hero results are deferred until pending reactions and death waves settle,
with the extra combat-preparation boundary above. Eye for an Eye can turn lethal
spell damage into a draw. No player-priority response or manual reaction action
is introduced: Hearthstone-style interruptions here resolve automatically.

REA-15 Weapon destruction queues last-known Deathrattles for both replacement
and exhaustion. Replacement installs the new weapon before draining the old
weapon callback. Truesilver's before-attack healing shares the preparation
queue with Secrets. Death's Bite's destruction callback precedes queued
AFTER_ATTACK callbacks, and can cause damage listeners and further deaths.
These scoped weapon timings retain the existing equip/durability profile;
Battlecry weapons, Gorehowl overrides and general replacement effects remain
deferred. The stage-3 immune-attacker durability policy is not changed here.

REA-16 max_reactions (default 1000) bounds all callback starts in a player
action, across nested damage and death waves. Existing max_deathrattles remains
a separate guard. Bounded reentrant callbacks are permitted; unbounded cycles
raise ResolutionLimitError atomically instead of implementing the supplied
historical self-trigger deferral rule. Input state, mana, uses, generated IDs,
events and RNG remain unchanged on failure. No private queue/counter remains
at a returned action boundary. scheduler.nextEventId survives JSON checkpoints.

Implementation and verification
-------------------------------
hearthstone/reactions.py: event snapshots, eligibility, continuation stack,
reaction recipients and interruptible combat. hearthstone/errors.py shares
the atomic resolution exception, still re-exported from prototype.py.
Prototype/MatchEngine/ZoneOperations publish the appropriate events and retain
public action signatures. Public players cannot activate reactions for free.

Tests: tests/test_reactions.py adds 52 cases (8 catalog/negative checks,
16 listener cases, 19 Secret cases, 5 weapon/interruption cases, 3 safety cases,
1 complete game). The complete suite contains 382 tests. Includes old versions,
listener departure, depth-first ordering, mortally wounded targets, Secret caps,
spell scaling, deferred draw result, and budgets across nested death waves.

  python3 -m examples.play_reactions

outputs/stage9-demo.json saves seed 1: player 0 wins on turn 47, 192 actions,
991 events. The public action sequence replays identically through a JSON
checkpoint before every action, checking empty work queues and Secret/board
invariants. It exercises counters, redirection/cancellation, damage/heal
callbacks and weapon Deathrattles. Sandbox decks intentionally mix classes.
Stage-7 and stage-8 demos are pinned to their historical catalogs/traces.

Sources, decisions and remaining scope
--------------------------------------
Original supplied specification, section 7 EFF-01..11, section 6 combat,
section 10 SEC-01..06, END-01, ARCH-01/03/08. The source explicitly warns that
its depth-first ordering evidence came from an old patch. This release tests
its named subset/policies; it does not establish current client conformance.
Primary card/patch references reviewed:
https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/cards/113/
https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/cards/462-eye-for-an-eye/
https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23739023
The 21.6 notes discuss temporary Spell Damage being consumed on Secret play
rather than saved for activation; this implementation uses current Spell Damage
at activation, without implementing that separate temporary-bonus mechanic.

The language does not yet cover draw/discard listeners, prevention/replacement
layers, arbitrary damage/death event filters, all target-invalidity policies,
random interruption choices, self-trigger deferral, forced attacks or modern
card types. General board-state copying, cost layers, hidden views, format
legality and verified single-patch conformance remain future stages.
