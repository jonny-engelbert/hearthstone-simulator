Stage 5: healing, buffs, auras and silence
=========================================
Implemented: 2026-10-20
Current catalog: foundation-v5; schema: 1.5
Health modifier policy: CAP_CURRENT_ON_MAX_LOSS
Death policy remains DEATH_WAVES_FIFO.

STA-01: Add four exact historical fixture definitions, revision foundation-v5:
EX1_011 Voodoo Doctor: 1 mana, 2/1, Battlecry restore 2 Health.
CS2_092 Blessing of Kings: 4 mana, Paladin spell, give a minion +4/+4.
CS2_222 Stormwind Champion: 7 mana, 6/6, other friendly minions have +1/+1.
EX1_048 Spellbreaker: 4 mana, 4/3, Battlecry silence a minion.
Stormwind Champion deliberately uses its historical 6/6, not the later 7/7.
The ten prior definitions and all earlier files remain unchanged.

STA-02: Schema 1.5 adds HEAL, BUFF, SILENCE, FRIENDLY selectors and the AURA
ability shape. HEAL uses a nonnegative constant and existing bound/query
recipients. BUFF has nonnegative attack/health modifiers and PERMANENT duration.
At least one modifier must be positive. BUFF/SILENCE require minion recipients.
AURA requires a friendly-minion selector and positive flat stat modifiers.
Reject unsupported fields/durations, negative/fractional/boolean modifiers,
unknown policies, no-op buffs/auras and mismatched AURA mechanic labels.

STA-03: HEAL reduces accumulated damage by min(requested, existing damage),
never below zero. It adds neither max Health nor Armor. It does not receive
Spell Damage, consume Divine Shield, or run damage prevention. Selected-target
legality still applies before execution, including enemy Immune/Stealth rules.
A full-Health legal target produces a HEAL event with amount zero. Healing an
enemy character is permitted when the card selector allows it. A queued heal
may save a lethally damaged character before the final death/result checkpoint;
it does not cancel pendingDestroy.

STA-04: A permanent buff appends a structured STAT_BUFF enchantment with source
instance ID and additive attack/health values. It persists after the spell or
granting source leaves play. Printed definitions are immutable. Buffs stack.
Current Attack feeds normal combat; max Health feeds damage/death checks.
No attack counts/readiness are refreshed by buffs or healing.

STA-05: Supported minion stats are printed values + attached STAT_BUFF values
+ contributions from all active flat AURA abilities. Aura sources are in-play
unsilenced minions; mortally wounded/pending-destroy sources remain active until
their death batch is removed. Each aura selector runs relative to its source's
current controller. SELF exclusion implements Stormwind's "other" clause.
Heroes/weapons do not receive these minion auras. Multiple Champions stack and
benefit one another. Incoming minions immediately receive existing auras.

STA-06: Auras are computed from current sources, not attached as permanent
enchantments. Re-evaluation never accumulates duplicate bonuses. Sources in
hand/graveyard or transformed/silenced sources contribute nothing. A fixture
control change moves the aura's affected set to the new controller. There is
no mind-control action yet. External auras still affect a silenced recipient.

STA-07: Keep accumulated damage plus max Health, with explicit adjustment when
a modifier is removed. On max-Health gain, leave damage unchanged: current
Health rises by the gain. On max-Health loss, preserve current Health up to the
new maximum. Algebraically: damage = max(0, damage + newMax - oldMax) on a loss.
This implements the supplied DOM-04 prose rather than assuming unchanged damage.
Examples: 9 max/3 damage (current 6) loses +4 Health => 5 max/0 damage, current 5.
9 max/8 damage (current 1) loses +4 => 5 max/4 damage, current 1.
A minion already at zero/negative current Health stays lethal under max loss.
This is a named tested fixture policy, not verification of every live stat layer.

STA-08: effectiveStats caches normalized Attack/max Health on in-play minions.
Refresh before stat-dependent actions, after relevant effects/board changes and at
death checkpoints. Refresh after removing a death batch, before its Deathrattles.
This lets nested damage see the remaining board's stats. Capture death snapshots
with the aura-influenced stats that existed when that batch was collected.
Whole ability / whole combat damage checkpoint boundaries remain as in stage 4.

STA-09: SILENCE clears current keywords and attached enchantments, sets silenced,
and suppresses intrinsic AURA and DEATHRATTLE abilities. Preserve identity,
damage subject to STA-07, controller, attack count, readiness and pendingDestroy.
Do not clear other minions' auras or permanent buffs previously granted to others.
New buffs may be added after silence; a later silence clears those new buffs.
No silence-proof keyword exceptions are implemented. Battlecries on played
hand cards follow the existing play path; full zone-reset behavior is deferred.

STA-10: TRANSFORM still creates destination stats at full Health, clears attached
buffs and intrinsic silenced state/keywords, and stamps a new play order. Apply
existing external auras afresh to the destination. The old aura/Deathrattle
identity does not survive transformation. A source disappearing removes its
aura; its effects already attached to other minions remain.

STA-11: STATS_CHANGED logs before/after stat values and damage adjustment;
HEAL logs actual restoration; BUFF logs source/target/modifiers; SILENCE logs
source/target. Stateless queries current_attack(state, entity),
max_health(entity, state) and current_health(state, entity) do not mutate state.
max_health(entity) without state uses normalized cached stats, or attached
stats if not yet normalized; pass state to include freshly arranged auras.
Normal actions return copies. Invalid requests or unsupported stat annotations
raise without committing partial updates. Results remain JSON-serializable.

STA-12: Scope is permanent positive additive minion stats and flat friendly
minion auras. Legacy string enchantments in old fixtures are opaque annotations;
they do not execute. Structured STAT_BUFF records are checked before use.
Negative/set-to/cost modifiers, temporary durations, keyword grants, adjacency,
tribe-filtered/conditional auras, Spell Damage auras, healing multipliers,
healing-to-damage conversion and Overheal listeners are deferred. Flat constants
avoid recursive dependencies between aura sources and recipients.

API example (run from project root)
------------------------------------
from hearthstone.catalog import load
from hearthstone.prototype import Prototype

engine = Prototype(load())
state = engine.state()
yeti = engine.entity('yeti', 'CS2_182', 0, 'PLAY')
yeti['damage'] = 3
kings = engine.entity('kings', 'CS2_092', 0, 'HAND')
state['entities'].extend([yeti, kings])
state = engine.play(state, 0, 'kings', 'yeti')
target = next(e for e in state['entities'] if e['id'] == 'yeti')
# Attack 8, max Health 9, current Health 6; printed definition is still 4/5.
assert engine.current_attack(state, target) == 8
assert engine.max_health(target, state) == 9
assert engine.current_health(state, target) == 6

        Spell Damage and Divine Shield independence; synthetic spell and
        Deathrattle healing (including saving a hero before final result), same-ability
        rescue of a mortally wounded minion and pendingDestroy persistence.
STA-04: two Kings stack, granting spell goes to graveyard, definition isolation,
        no attack refresh, buffed stats used in combat and targeting atomicity.
STA-05/06: source exclusion, friendly filter, heroes unaffected, incoming minion,
           mutual Champions, separate buff/aura layers, source silence, fixture
           controller/zone changes and idempotent refresh.
STA-07: retain damage on Health increase; cap current Health on modifier loss;
        preserve low current Health; already-lethal simultaneous deaths stay dead.
STA-08: aura raises lethal threshold; source removal before synthetic
        Deathrattle damage creates the expected next wave.
STA-09: real Spellbreaker removes buffs, Taunt/Frozen and Loot Deathrattle;
        external aura remains and silenced minion accepts new buffs.
STA-10: Polymorph clears buffs and receives external aura; transforming an aura
        source removes its contribution without old identity effects.
STA-11/12: read-only stat queries, no accumulation, malformed stat annotation
           atomicity, STATS_CHANGED events, deterministic JSON roundtrip.
