Stage 7: hero powers, combat keywords and turn-boundary effects
=============================================================
2026-10-20; foundation-v7; schema 1.7; historical-foundation-fixtures.
Previous catalog/schema/manifest files are preserved byte-for-byte. Earlier
card definitions retain exact revisions. The catalog now contains 23 entries.

Representative definitions
--------------------------
CS2_034 Fireblast: 2 mana, HERO_POWER, Mage; selected character takes 1 damage.
CS1h_001 Lesser Heal: 2 mana, HERO_POWER, Priest; selected character heals 2.
CS2_124 Wolfrider: 3 mana, 3/1, Charge.
GIL_580 Rabid Worgen: 3 mana, 3/3 Warrior, Rush, The Witchwood.
EX1_033 Windfury Harpy: 6 mana, 4/5, Windfury.
CS2_024 Frostbolt: 2 mana, selected character takes 3 spell damage, then Freeze.
EX1_046 Abusive Sergeant: historical 1 mana 2/1; targeted Battlecry gives a
minion +2 Attack until the end of the current turn. This is the pre-6.1.3 body.

This deliberately mixes historical fixtures and a later Rush representative;
it is not one live-client patch snapshot. Display text is never interpreted.
All new definitions have revision foundation-v7. No Google source was changed.

Requirements and execution contract
-----------------------------------
KEY-01 Hero powers are noncollectible HERO_POWER definitions, instantiated in
PLAY outside the minion board, hand and deck. new_game(..., hero_powers=[...])
accepts two IDs, exact {cardId, revision} references, or None to disable one.
Default v7 setup uses Fireblast for both players. Older catalogs without power
definitions default to no power. This sandbox still permits mixed-class decks;
configuring a power does not validate hero/deck class or format legality.

KEY-02 hero_power(state, player, target_id=None), or apply with
{type: hero_power, target: entityID}, requires the active player's MAIN phase,
an unfinished match, a configured power, full available mana and an unused power.
Reject invalid requests atomically. Charge mana and consume one use even if
healing restores zero or damage is absorbed. Reset only at that player's next
start of turn. It does not consume a hand slot, emit PLAY, or increment the
cards-played counter. Resolution includes existing death and result checkpoints.

KEY-03 HERO_POWER_EFFECT uses HERO_POWER_SELECTED_TARGET. Both spell and power
selected targeting exclude Elusive, enemy Stealth and enemy Immune. Friendly
Stealth remains targetable. Taunt does not restrict powers. HERO_POWER damage
does not inherit Spell Damage. Armor, Divine Shield and immunity still apply.
Frozen heroes can use powers and equip weapons. Powers cannot attack or be
played as hand cards. Passive powers, upgrades, refresh effects, discounts and
summoning powers are deferred with later card operations and scheduler work.

KEY-04 Charge permits immediate attacks against legal enemy characters. Rush
permits immediate attacks against minions while normal readiness is absent.
After the next own-turn refresh, a Rush minion may attack heroes too. Charge
takes precedence when both are present. Both obey Taunt, Freeze, positive
Attack, ownership, health and attack limits. Readiness is not set by a keyword;
silencing away Charge/Rush restores normal exhaustion on the arrival turn.

KEY-05 Windfury increases the attack limit from one to two. Attack counters
belong to the character, never the keyword or weapon. Gaining Windfury after
one attack makes a second available; losing it does not refund any attack.
Hero combat reads Windfury from the equipped weapon instance. Replacement
retains the hero's count; exhaustion or replacement removes the old weapon's
contribution. The catalog contains a Windfury minion, not a scripted Windfury
weapon; injected weapon keywords exercise this generic branch. Mega-Windfury,
forced attacks and general keyword-granting operations are outside this stage.

KEY-06 FREEZE is an explicit character effect, separate from its damage. A
frozen character cannot declare attacks and still retaliates normally. Freeze
is not dependent on the preceding damage penetrating Armor or a shield.

KEY-07 Apply supplied KW-05 as the fixture policy: on the controller's own turn,
if no attack has been spent and a legal attack is available ignoring Freeze,
thaw at this turn's end. Otherwise thaw at the end of the next own turn.
Opponent-turn Freeze also expires at the end of the recipient's next own turn.
This covers exhausted minions, unarmed heroes, Rush without legal minion targets,
and Windfury after its first attack. The latter deliberately follows the
supplied specification's after-any-attack rule; no client-log verification of
that fine timing is claimed. Repeated Freeze does not add skipped turns;
retain the later applicable deadline, anchored to the controller at application.
Control-change and forced-attack interactions are deferred with those operations.

KEY-08 Store freezeUntil {player, playerTurn}, using own-turn counters. Thaw
after end hooks, end-of-turn buffs and temporary mana expiry, before handover.
Never thaw a graveyard character. Silence removes Frozen and its deadline;
transform creates a fresh destination state without the old deadline.

KEY-09 BUFF durations are PERMANENT, END_OF_TURN or START_OF_NEXT_TURN. A timed
STAT_BUFF stores an expiration record. END_OF_TURN anchors to the current
global turn, even if the recipient is an enemy. START_OF_NEXT_TURN anchors to
the effect controller's next own-turn count, independent of the recipient or
source's subsequent state. Timers do not depend on the source staying alive.

KEY-10 Resolve end hooks with buffs still present, then remove due buffs,
refresh stats, settle deaths and check results. Before start hooks, remove
start-due buffs, settle stats/deaths/results, then refresh attacks/resources
and proceed with the existing start hooks and draw. Health loss uses existing
CAP_CURRENT_ON_MAX_LOSS, not subtraction from current Health. Flat external
auras and permanent enchantments survive expiration. No attack is refunded.
Silence/transform discard attached timers. General hero-stat buffs remain
outside the supported minion BUFF operation.

KEY-11 State and action processing remains copy-on-write. Invalid actions and
failed resolution never commit partial payments, use counts, timers or events.
New timer state is ordinary JSON. Restoring a JSON checkpoint retains exact
future expiration. Prototype has no timing clock: effectful FREEZE/temporary
BUFF requires a managed match; legacy combat fixture helpers do not thaw buffs.

Validation and trace coverage
-----------------------------
tests/test_keywords.py contains 53 new tests:
- Five catalog/schema tests, including all seven frozen manifests and previous
  definition continuity, negative power shape/class/policy/duration cases.
- Eleven power tests: costs/limits, own-turn reset, legality/atomicity, freeze,
  healing, armor/shield, lethal and Loot Hoarder, setup/dispatch and replay.
- Nine combat keyword tests: Charge, Rush, Taunt, silence, Windfury refresh,
  gain/loss, hero weapon replacement and durability.
- Sixteen Freeze tests: actual Frostbolt and isolated zero-damage timing
  fixtures, own/opponent turns, exhausted/Charge/Rush/Windfury, repeated Freeze,
  unarmed/armed heroes, retaliation, silence/transform and graveyard handling.
- Eleven buff expiration tests: enemy targets, permanent layers/auras, phase
  ordering, next-own-turn anchoring, Health clamping, source death, cleanup,
  attack counts and JSON/atomicity.
- One complete public-action game, replayed through a JSON checkpoint before
  every action. Tests use in-memory mutations and arranged resources where
  needed to isolate rules; these are not free production player actions.

The complete suite contains 275 tests. See validation-results.txt for saved
output. examples/play_keywords.py writes outputs/stage7-demo.json. Seed 0 uses
all six principal event categories: HERO_POWER, FREEZE, THAW, BUFF_EXPIRED,
ATTACK and DURABILITY. It completes at turn 17 in 79 public actions; player 1
wins. Decks are sandbox fixtures. The example policy is not an AI opponent.
