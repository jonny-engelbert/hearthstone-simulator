Layer 1: Structural validation
------------------------------
Check JSON Schema itself against Draft 2020-12. Accept all 51 current definitions and all eight preserved catalogs.
Reject unknown fields, operations, policies and schema versions; negative mana;
nonpositive minion Health; missing minion stats; and stats on spells.
Tests: CatalogTests.test_schema_itself_is_valid,
test_unknown_fields_and_operations_are_rejected,
test_invalid_stat_shapes_are_rejected.
Requirements: FND-04, FND-05.

Layer 2: Semantic validation
----------------------------
Check reviewed ID/name mappings, unique (ID, revision) pairs, bound targets,
exact token revisions, transform destination type, ability/type compatibility,
damage classification, and recipient/batch compatibility.
Tests: all remaining CatalogTests methods.
Requirements: FND-01, FND-03, FND-06.
The ID/name regression oracle is separate from generic schema validation:
a structurally valid arbitrary ID is not evidence of a real Hearthstone card.

Layer 3: Executable fixture scenarios
-------------------------------------
BehaviorTests uses manually arranged states and explicit expected outcomes.
It executes the catalog through the same generic interpreter used by the
prototype, without card-name or card-ID branches in execution code.

Scenario families and requirement traceability:
- Vanilla play, exhaustion, mana, full board and wrong turn: FND-10.
- Definition independence and distinct copies: FND-01, FND-07.
- Required/conditional/optional targeting and illegal-action atomicity:
  FND-08, FND-09, FND-10.
- Battlecry self exclusion and no-valid-target branch: FND-08, FND-09.
- Spell versus Battlecry targeting, Stealth and Elusive: FND-09.
- Battlecry versus Spell Damage, Divine Shield and Armor: FND-12, FND-13.
- Area recipients, per-recipient bonus and grouped lethal removal:
  FND-11, FND-12, FND-14.
- Area damage bypasses selection protections but respects Immune: FND-09,13.
- Polymorph clears damage/buffs and preserves control without a death:
  FND-15 (fixture policy only).
- Taunt limits attack choices but not Fireball: FND-09.
- Empty-board Flamestrike remains playable: FND-08, FND-11.
- Identical inputs return identical state/log output: FND-10.
- Lethal damage finalizes the fixture and blocks further actions: FND-16.

Artificial fixture details
--------------------------
Zero-target Fire Elemental is tested by moving the friendly hero to SET_ASIDE
and making the enemy hero Immune. This is intentionally synthetic branch
coverage, not a claim that such a board occurs in ordinary matches.
OPTIONAL mode is tested using a modified in-memory definition, not presented
as the real Fire Elemental's behavior. Shield/Elusive/Stealth/Immune states are
injected as test inputs; the catalog need not contain their granting cards.
spellDamage is injected; general Spell Damage auras remain deferred. Flat
Attack/Health auras are implemented and tested in stage 5.

Limits of current evidence
--------------------------
These checks establish internal consistency and selected interpreter behavior.
They do not establish full Hearthstone correctness or current patch fidelity.
There are no client-log differential tests, full-game fuzz tests,
hidden views or general reaction-listener tests. Remaining gaps also include
advanced enchantment layers, scripted weapon behavior, reactive draw listeners,
weapon Deathrattles, resurrection or conditional/adjacent auras.
FND-02 manifest integrity is checked by the dedicated manifest command below.
Full instance-ID uniqueness validation is deferred with a public state schema.

Manifest integrity check:
  python3 -m hearthstone.manifest

Next acceptance gates
----------------------
1. Before expanding the catalog, choose an explicit historical client snapshot
   or a verified current build. Import metadata and retain provenance/hashes.
2. Add versioned public state/action/event schemas and invariant checks.
3. COMPLETE: draw, fatigue and full-hand behavior with Novice Engineer tests.
4. COMPLETE: normal attack execution and plain weapons with Fiery War Axe tests.
5. COMPLETE: death-wave scheduling, Loot Hoarder and nested minion deaths.
   General event listeners and other reaction windows remain future work.
6. Add seeded random-hit tests only after defining hit count scaling and when
   random recipients are selected again (Arcane Missiles).
Stage 5 COMPLETE: capped healing, additive permanent buffs, flat auras and silence.
Stage 6 COMPLETE: setup, mulligans, full supported turns and resource handling.
Three planned stages remain; see roadmap.txt.

7. Verify uncertain rules against client logs, recording build and expected
   event order. Only then claim conformance to that build.

Stage 7 COMPLETE: 53 new tests in tests/test_keywords.py; 275 tests total.
Coverage and KEY-01..11 mapping: docs/keywords-stage7.txt. All seven catalogs
and manifests are validated, with old card definitions unchanged. New cases
cover hero-power costs/targeting/usage resets, Charge/Rush/Windfury limits,
weapon-count persistence, KW-05 Freeze deadlines, buff expiration at both
turn boundaries, cleanup and JSON checkpoint determinism. A complete mixed
card game is replayed through the public action dispatcher and JSON restore
before every action. These checks do not establish general save-state schema
validation or live-client conformance.

Stage 8 COMPLETE: 55 new tests in tests/test_zones.py; 330 tests total.
ZON-01..14 coverage is detailed in zones-stage8.txt. Structural tests reject
invalid generation references, policies, counts, contexts and recipient types.
Behavior tests cover chosen/default positioning, partial/full board summons,
power legality, ID allocation, auras, exhausted/Charge/Rush tokens, generation
into full hands, bounce cleanup/replay, base copies, deterministic shuffles,
fatigue preservation, 99-card cap and Deathrattle overflow behavior. Nested
generation respects FIFO waves and atomic budget failure. A 177-action public
game replays through JSON checkpoints with zone and capacity invariants at
every step. All eight frozen manifests and prefix definition continuity pass.
These checks establish declared fixture behavior, not live-client parity.

Stage 9 COMPLETE: 52 new tests in tests/test_reactions.py; 382 total.
REA-01..16 are documented in reactions-stage9.txt. Cases cover immutable
listener capture, nested depth-first ordering, queued-source departure/silence,
pre-action After barriers, damage-batch timing, actual healing, fatal Acolyte
draws, Cult Master/Deathrattle chronology, Secret slots/ownership timing,
Counterspell and Overload suppression, live Secret damage scaling, Noble
redirection, Vaporize attacker destruction, targetless Secret continuation,
Eye-for-an-Eye lethal draws, weapon healing/destruction and action-wide budgets.
The 192-action sandbox game replays through JSON checkpoints, checking Secret
uniqueness, capacity and absence of pending work after every action. All nine
manifests and prefix continuity are checked. Broader client-log comparisons,
draw/discard listeners, prevention layers and per-player hidden views remain.
