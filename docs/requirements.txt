Reviewed card definition and execution contract
==============================================
Date: 2026-10-20
Current schema: 1.6; catalog: foundation-v6
Earlier stages: schemas 1.1 through 1.5; catalogs foundation-v1 through v5
Profile: historical-foundation-fixtures
Status: implemented prototype requirements, separate from full engine roadmap.

1. Scope and versioning

FND-01: A definition is keyed by (cardId, revision). Instance IDs identify
individual objects in a match and must never be used as catalog identifiers.
Preserve upstream IDs; never invent suffixes to conceal collisions.

FND-02: The catalog envelope declares schemaVersion, catalogId, rulesProfile
and basis. The manifest records exact catalog bytes with SHA-256. Definitions
are copied into the interpreter, so caller edits cannot mutate its catalog.
Do not overwrite a published revision to represent different gameplay.

FND-03: All foundation catalogs are deliberately historical test fixtures, not a complete
historical patch snapshot or current Standard/Wild card pool. Fire Elemental
deals 3 and Flamestrike deals 4. Spell schools and Fire Elemental's later-added
Elemental tribe are intentionally absent. Text and effect constants agree.
The fixture does not claim current card metadata or legal constructed decks.

2. Definition structure

FND-04: Every definition provides name, type, classes, baseStats, collectible,
set, rarity, tribes, spellSchool, keywords, mechanics, localized text,
playConditions, abilities, and source links. In this narrow historical profile
basic cards use set=BASIC and rarity=FREE; Loot Hoarder uses
set=EXPERT1 and rarity=COMMON. Zero mana and zero Attack are valid; minion Health
must be positive. Spells cannot contain Attack, Health or durability. Weapons require Attack and
positive durability, have no Health, and do not occupy minion board slots.

FND-05: JSON Schema validates structure, required fields and supported enums.
Unknown fields, schema versions, operations and policies fail closed.
The prototype supports MINION, SPELL and plain WEAPON, the fixture's classes and keywords,
and at most one ability of each supported kind per definition: BATTLECRY,
DEATHRATTLE, AURA, START_OF_TURN and END_OF_TURN on minions; SPELL_EFFECT on spells.
Weapons have no abilities in this stage. Adding other
types requires schema, semantic validator, interpreter, documentation and tests.
Do not confuse the narrow supported schema with the complete future domain.

FND-06: Semantic validation rejects duplicate definition keys, dangling or
wrong-type transform references, unbound target names, incompatible ability
and damage classes, conflicting targeting policies and inconsistent mechanic
labels. Polymorph refers to Sheep by both cardId and revision.

3. Runtime state and targeting

FND-07: Instances store owner and controller separately, zone, definition
reference, damage, armor, mutable keywords, enchantments and readiness.
Characters track attacksThisTurn; weapon instances track current durability.
Minions/weapon entries carry globally unique playOrder stamps. State records
nextPlayOrder and immutable-at-capture deathHistory snapshots.
Printed stats come from the definition. Permanent positive STAT_BUFF entries
add Attack/Health; active flat AURA abilities add separate contributions.
effectiveStats caches normalized values, with an explicit Health adjustment
policy on max-Health loss. Legacy fixture strings remain opaque annotations.
State and event logs are JSON-serializable dictionaries. Runtime fixture state
is trusted test input, not a validated public interchange format.

FND-08: REQUIRED demands one legal target; absence rejects play.
REQUIRED_IF_AVAILABLE demands a target if any legal candidate exists, and
otherwise allows no target. OPTIONAL allows a legal target or no target.
The ability's targeting object is the single source of target requirements.
playConditions must be empty until independent predicates are implemented.

FND-09: A selector declares zone, entityKind, controller relation and optional
self exclusion. Enemy relations are relative to the effect controller.
SPELL_SELECTED_TARGET excludes enemy Stealth and Immune and all Elusive
characters. BATTLECRY_SELECTED_TARGET excludes enemy Stealth and Immune but
permits Elusive. Taunt restricts attack selection, not these abilities.
Friendly Stealth and Immune do not prevent selected targeting; Immune still
prevents damage. These are explicitly scoped fixture policies.

FND-10: A source must be in the acting player's hand, with enough mana, on that
player's decision point. Minion play requires a free slot out of seven.
Invalid play raises an error and changes neither the input state nor its log.
Valid play returns a new state, pays mana, and places a minion exhausted or
resolves a spell before moving it to the graveyard. Weapon play equips it and
replaces any prior weapon under the same controller (see WPN requirements).

4. Effects and damage

FND-11: DEAL_DAMAGE has a constant amount, recipient, missingRecipient=SKIP,
application and damageClass. BOUND_TARGET uses a declared binding. QUERY
captures an affected set at EFFECT_START. Affected-set queries do not inherit
selected-target restrictions. Missing bound recipients skip the effect.

FND-12: SINGLE_HIT uses a bound target. SINGLE_DAMAGE_BATCH uses a query.
Compute the spell bonus and deliver that amount separately to every recipient.
For 4 base damage and +1 Spell Damage, each affected minion takes 5 before
prevention. Batching controls timing, not bonus distribution.

FND-13: SPELL damage receives the controller's fixture spellDamage bonus.
ABILITY damage does not. Both pass through Immune, Divine Shield, and Armor
before increasing damage. Divine Shield is consumed only on a positive hit.
NO_MODIFIERS is intentionally unsupported; source category must not bypass
shared defenses. spellDamage is a fixture input, not an implemented aura.

FND-14: All damage in a batch occurs before removing lethal minions together.
The fixture emits PLAY, DAMAGE, DEATH_BATCH and TRANSFORM events, plus
DEATHRATTLE and DESTROY in stage 4. Death checkpoints follow whole abilities
and paired combat damage. Each wave removes all dying minions, captures
Deathrattles in play order, drains that queue, then collects the next wave.
General reaction windows and damage-trigger listeners are not implemented.
This explicit fixture policy does not establish live-client timing conformance.

FND-15: TRANSFORM replaces the definition with the referenced minion, clears
damage, armor, keywords and attached modifiers, restores destination keywords,
and preserves owner, controller and list position. The fixture retains the
instance ID, resets readiness, and emits TRANSFORM without SUMMON or DEATH.
Full entity identity, play-order stamping and readiness semantics require
live-client verification before implementing the complete transform policy.

FND-16: After resolving a fixture play, check hero damage and emit a final
winner/draw marker. Reject further plays once the result is final. There are
no damage replacement effects currently implemented.

5. Future engine contract (not implemented)

The original engine specification remains a roadmap after corrections recorded
in review-and-decisions.txt. Remaining full-engine work includes draw-trigger reactions,
advanced combat interruptions, random card effects, pending choices, legal-action enumeration,
format legality, hidden views, save/restore, advanced stat layers, enchantment
transitions, advanced weapon scripts, advanced hero powers, locations, replacement effects,
quests, additional event families, board-state copies and random generated pools.

Do not declare an operation supported merely because its enum is added.
Stage-2 extension: requirements DRW-01..12 in drawing-stage2.txt add DRAW_CARD,
ordered deck instance IDs, successful draws, full-hand burns and fatigue.
Novice Engineer uses a new foundation-v2 definition revision; unchanged cards
retain their original revisions. All nine versioned schemas/catalogs are supported.
The legacy Prototype fixture has no turn runner; managed matches now use MatchEngine.

Every extension must ship structural tests, semantic negative tests and
behavioral scenarios. Every historical or uncertain timing policy needs
recorded evidence and a versioned policy before claiming live-game parity.

Stage-3 extension: WPN-01..12 in combat-stage3.txt define plain weapon equip,
replacement, normal declared attacks, paired damage, attack counters and
durability. The combat-turn primitive refreshes incoming-player readiness only;
thaw/temporary-modifier phases are implemented in stage 7; managed mana/draw/turn hooks
are now implemented in stage 6. Fiery War Axe uses
revision foundation-v3; other definitions keep their previous exact revisions.

Stage-4 extension: DTH-01..12 in deaths-stage4.txt define death snapshots,
controller-at-death, play-order stamps, an immutable per-wave queue, iterative
nested resolution and a bounded atomic failure mode. Generic DESTROY marks
minions for collection at the next checkpoint. Loot Hoarder uses revision
foundation-v4; earlier artifacts remain unchanged. Weapons with Deathrattles,
general event listeners, summons/resurrection and advanced auras remain future work.

Stage-5 extension: STA-01..12 in stats-stage5.txt define healing, positive
permanent minion stat buffs, flat friendly-minion auras and minion silence.
Normalize stats after board/effect changes and before death checks. The named
CAP_CURRENT_ON_MAX_LOSS policy preserves current Health on max-Health loss
up to the new cap, adjusting accumulated damage. Hero Health and Armor remain
separate; healing changes damage only. Cost/set-stat modifiers, keyword
grants and conditional/adjacent auras remain future work. See roadmap.txt.

Stage-6 extension: MAT-01..16 in match-stage6.txt define the managed match
lifecycle, deterministic setup RNG, atomic mulligans, start/main/end phases,
automatic draws, mana buckets, temporary mana expiry, Overload and match limits.
START_OF_TURN/END_OF_TURN use owner-scoped ordered queues. MatchEngine player
actions reject fixture shortcuts and require managed states. Deck profile is
FOUNDATION_SANDBOX: size and collectible definitions checked; class/copy/format
legality follows in a later stage. CAPPED_TEMPORARY_MANA replaces the original
spec's unverified 11-of-10 temporary-mana example for this historical profile.

Stage-7 extension: KEY-01..11 in keywords-stage7.txt define hero-power actions,
separate targeting/damage classes, Charge/Rush/Windfury combat limits, explicit
Freeze deadlines, and timed minion buff expiration. Managed start/end phases
perform resets and expiration. Preserve supplied KW-05 as a fixture policy,
including next-own-turn thaw after any attack; do not claim client parity.
Hero power configuration does not enforce deck classes. Timed modifiers use
current global turn or effect controller's next own turn, independent of
recipient/source lifetime. All new definitions and schemas use version 1.7.

Stage-8 extension: ZON-01..14 in zones-stage8.txt define ordered board placement,
SUMMON, ADD_CARD, RETURN_TO_HAND, SHUFFLE_INTO_DECK and SHUFFLE_COPIES. New
entities use deterministic serialized IDs. Movement retains instance identity
and owner but resets board effects; deck copies use the current exact base
definition, not board state. Full-hand bounce destroys in play and preserves
Deathrattle eligibility. Virtual death slots preserve summon positioning within
FIFO death waves. Catalog/schema/manifest name RESET_ON_EXIT_BASE_DECK_COPIES.
The 99-card cap follows the supplied spec; destruction on failed full-deck
shuffle remains an explicit unverified fixture policy. Earlier catalogs retain
legacy placement/event behavior. Reactive listeners are the next stage.

Stage-9 extension: REA-01..16 in reactions-stage9.txt define TRIGGER/SECRET
abilities, event-time immutable listener queues, source revalidation, typed
event recipients and bounded depth-first delivery. Card play publishes before/
after windows; damage/heal/summon/death observations use the same scheduler.
Countered spells pay but skip text, Overload and After Spell. Secrets occupy
five distinct slots and activate only off-turn. Combat preparation supports
cancellation and fixed Defender redirection, followed by paired combat damage.
Weapons support before-attack scripts and last-known destruction Deathrattles.
DEPTH_FIRST_REACTIONS_DEATH_WAVES_V1 records the explicit historical fixture
choices, including cancellation refund and bounded reentrant callbacks rather
than the supplied obsolete self-deferral/two-death-phase attack-count rules.
No client-patch conformance or per-player Secret redaction is claimed yet.
