Remaining milestones after stage 10
==================================
Estimate updated 2026-10-21: two planned stages; reasonable range one to three.
Target: a useful headless foundational simulator, not the entire card collection.
Stages 1-10 and the hardening pass are implemented. The current catalog contains
60 definitions. Stage 10 adds seeded random outcomes, private generation, Discover,
Choose One, bounded JSON-resumable atomic decision transactions and complete-game
replay. Uniform catalog-scoped pools and shared-target choices are explicit
fixture policies; client animation timing is not modeled.

11. Deck legality and stable integration contracts
    Pin supported deck/class/format rules; validate constructed decks. Formalize
    action/event/view/checkpoint schemas and compatibility policy; provide a
    small public client/AI integration example using the supported APIs.

12. Pinned-profile conformance
    Choose one explicit client/card revision profile, broaden representative
    decks, compare client logs and declare a reproducible conformance matrix.
    Extend Discover eligibility/weighting and choice targeting as that pool needs.
    Continue invariant/performance checks on the larger supported pool.

These may merge if the target stays small; client conformance may need a split.
No delivery-date promise. Full Hearthstone coverage, modern card types and
advanced stat/replacement layers remain additional scope.
