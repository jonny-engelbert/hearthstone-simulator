Remaining milestones after the pre-stage-10 hardening pass
=========================================================
Estimate updated 2026-10-21: three planned stages; reasonable range two to four.
Target: a useful headless foundational simulator, not the entire card collection.
Stages 1-9 and the hardening pass are implemented. Legal-action enumeration,
per-player views, random legal-game invariants and modest performance groundwork
were pulled forward from the former stage-11/12 plan.

10. Random effects and pausable choices
    Random targets/card pools, Discover/Choose One, typed pending choices and
    deterministic resume. Add private generation/choice visibility and replay
    tests before exposing those events in views.

11. Deck legality and stable integration contracts
    Pin supported deck/class/format rules; validate constructed decks. Formalize
    action/event/view/checkpoint schemas and compatibility policy; provide a
    small public client/AI integration example using the new APIs.

12. Pinned-profile conformance
    Choose one explicit client/card revision profile, broaden representative
    decks, compare client logs and declare a reproducible conformance matrix.
    Continue invariant/performance checks on the larger supported pool.

Stages 11/12 may merge if the target remains small; choice scheduling or client
conformance may need a split. No delivery-date promise. Full Hearthstone coverage,
modern card types and advanced stat/replacement layers are additional scope.
