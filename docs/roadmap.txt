Remaining project stages after stage 9
=====================================
Estimate made: 2026-10-20
Working plan: three more stages. Updated planning range: two to five.
The prior estimate after stage 8 was four planned, range three to six.
This is an engineering estimate, not a delivery-date commitment.

Target milestone: a useful headless foundational simulator that can set up a
match, run complete turns with a deliberately supported card subset, enumerate
legal actions, preserve hidden information, and replay deterministic games.
Full Hearthstone card coverage is a continuing content/conformance effort.
Current release has a managed match loop with 51 representative definitions, plus
the legacy fixture interpreter. Constructed legality and public hidden views
remain deferred.

Completed
-----------
1. Card language, validation, targeting, simple damage/transformation.
2. Drawing, fatigue, full-hand burns and Novice Engineer.
3. Fiery War Axe, normal combat, weapon replacement and durability.
4. Loot Hoarder, snapshots, ordered Deathrattles and nested death waves.
5. Healing, additive permanent buffs, flat auras and silence cleanup.
6. Managed setup, seeded shuffle, mulligans, The Coin, full supported turns,
   mana/Overload bookkeeping, owner-scoped phase hooks and match limits.

7. Hero powers, Charge/Rush/Windfury, Freeze deadlines and timed minion buffs.
   Separate power actions, own-turn use resets, end/start expiration, seven
   representative definitions and complete public-action replay coverage.

8. Ordered minion placement, token/Deathrattle summons, generated hand cards,
   bounce, seeded shuffles, clean deck copies and explicit zone reset policies.
   Reinforce and 13 further definitions; complete public-action replay and
   capacity/identity/zone invariants. Board-state copying remains deferred.

9. Bounded depth-first reactions, event snapshots, play/damage/heal/summon/death
   listeners, Secrets, weapon scripts and attack/spell interruption windows.
   Fourteen new definitions, fixed attack redirection/cancellation and complete
   public-action replay. Additional event families and replacement layers remain.

Proposed remaining sequence
----------------------------
10. Random effects and pausable choices.
    Extend the setup RNG to random targets/card pools, Discover/Choose One,
    typed pending-choice requests and deterministic resume behavior.
    Acceptance: replay/branch identical states and seeds through choices.

11. Deck rules and stable public engine APIs.
    Pinned catalog/format legality, hero/class restrictions, legal-action
    enumeration, per-player hidden views, snapshot/restore, action/event schemas
    and replay contracts. Some API work should be introduced incrementally earlier.
    Acceptance: a client/AI can drive the engine using supported public actions.

12. Foundational conformance and hardening.
    Choose and verify one explicit client/card revision profile, add a broader
    supported deck pool, client-log comparisons, random legal-game invariant
    tests, replay/restore tests and measured performance work.
    Acceptance: a declared conformance matrix with reproducible full-game tests.

Why the range is two to five
-----------------------------
Some adjacent stages may merge; the reaction scheduler, zone transitions and
conformance work may each expose enough historical rules to split. Advanced
card types (locations/hero replacement/quests), complex stat layers and modern
keywords will need further work if added to the milestone's scope.
Full turns are now implemented for the supported catalog; re-estimate again
after the random-effect/choice stage. Stage count
measures milestones rather than equal-sized units of time or effort.
