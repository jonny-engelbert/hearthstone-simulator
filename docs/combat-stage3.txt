Stage 3: Fiery War Axe, weapons, combat and durability
=====================================================
Implemented: 2026-10-20
Current catalog: foundation-v3; schema: 1.3
Status: plain weapons and normal declared combat in the fixture interpreter.

WPN-01: Fiery War Axe is CS2_106, revision foundation-v3, a Warrior WEAPON
costing 2 mana with 3 Attack and 2 durability. Use its original historical cost,
not the later 3-mana version from Gemini's examples. Keep the eight previous
definitions unchanged. Retain v1/v2 catalogs, schemas and manifests byte-for-byte.

WPN-02: Schema 1.3 supports WEAPON/WARRIOR and weapon baseStats containing
manaCost, attack and positive integer durability. Health is forbidden. Spells
and minions cannot contain durability. Weapons in this stage must have empty
abilities, keywords, mechanics and tribes. Reject scripted weapons until their
timing and reactions are implemented; a WEAPON enum alone is insufficient.

WPN-03: Playing a weapon checks turn, source control/hand, mana and absence of
an unexpected target, then pays mana and equips it in PLAY. It occupies no
minion slot. Exactly one equipped weapon per controller is supported. Another
weapon replaces the old one into GRAVEYARD with WEAPON_DESTROYED/REPLACED,
then emits EQUIP for the new instance. Replacement does not expend durability.
This event order is a plain-weapon fixture contract, not a specification of
weapon Battlecry/Deathrattle order. No such reactions exist in this catalog.

WPN-04: Current durability lives on the weapon instance; printed durability
remains in its definition. Hero current Attack is its fixture attack value
plus its equipped weapon's printed Attack on its own turn. Weapon Attack is
sheathed on the opponent's turn. Weapons themselves cannot attack or be normal
combat targets. Definition/instance isolation remains enforced.

WPN-05: Both heroes and minions may make a normal declared attack on their
controller's active turn if in PLAY, alive, ready, at positive current Attack,
not Frozen or CANT_ATTACK, and with attacksThisTurn < 1. Minions enter play
unready; heroes start ready. One attack per turn is the supported baseline.
Charge/Rush, Windfury, forced attacks and other attack-count overrides are
not implemented or permitted in card definitions. Fixture keyword injection
tests cover only stated protections/restrictions, not arbitrary new keywords.

WPN-06: Legal defenders are living enemy characters in PLAY without Stealth
or Immune. A visible, non-Immune enemy Taunt minion restricts normal attacks to
such Taunt minions. A Stealthed or Immune Taunt does not protect other targets.
Attack targeting and attack execution use the same checks. Own characters,
hand/graveyard entities, spells, weapons and missing IDs are invalid targets.

WPN-07: Consume the attack and remove attacker Stealth before combat damage.
Capture both combatants' Attack values before applying either hit. The attacker
deals its current Attack to the defender; a minion defender retaliates with
its current Attack even when lethally hit or unready/Frozen. A defending hero
never retaliates, even if armed or possessing fixture Attack. Zero-Attack
defenders produce no return-damage event. Spell Damage never modifies combat.

WPN-08: Apply combat hits using the shared Immune/Divine Shield/Armor/Health
pipeline, tagged damageClass=COMBAT and attributed to the dealing character.
Both hits resolve before lethal minions are removed together. Thus lethal
damage to a defender does not suppress its return hit. Log serialization lists
attacker damage first and return damage second; this is paired combat with no
death or result checks between the two, not full reactive event timing.

WPN-09: Every valid hero attack with an equipped plain weapon consumes one
durability, including damage prevented by Divine Shield and retaliation
prevented by hero Immune. This corrects the supplied spec's hero-Immune
exception. A minion attacking, an enemy attacking the hero, an invalid attack,
or replacing a weapon never consumes that weapon's durability. Durability
overrides from special weapon text are deferred.

WPN-10: At durability zero emit WEAPON_DESTROYED/EXHAUSTED and move the weapon
to GRAVEYARD. Its Attack no longer contributes. Hero attacksThisTurn belongs
to the hero and survives weapon replacement or destruction; new weapon equip
never grants a fresh attack. All combat damage and durability changes finish
before grouped minion death processing and the final hero win/loss check.
Lethal retaliation can kill the attacking hero while killing the defender.
Final results reject further play, attack, draw or combat-turn refresh calls.

WPN-11: begin_combat_turn(state, player) is a fixture/future-turn-runner
primitive, not a player action. It sets the active player, resets only that
player's in-play characters' attacksThisTurn, and marks them ready. It does
not refill mana, draw, thaw Frozen, expire buffs or run start/end-of-turn events.
Use it to arrange successive combat turns until a complete turn runner exists.
Do not expose it as an action that lets a player refresh attacks at will.

WPN-12: play, attack, and begin_combat_turn return copied states. Invalid
requests leave caller state, events, mana, attack counts and durability unchanged.
Invalid players, missing/duplicate combat entity IDs, multiple equipped weapons
or equipped weapons already at zero durability fail rather than guessing.
Normal supported states remain deterministic and JSON-serializable.

Events
--------
PLAY and EQUIP: weapon play/equip.
WEAPON_DESTROYED: reason REPLACED or EXHAUSTED, with weapon instance ID.
ATTACK: attacker and defender instance IDs, after validation.
DAMAGE: source character, target character, amount, damageClass=COMBAT.
DURABILITY: weapon instance ID and remaining durability.
DEATH_BATCH: all lethally damaged minions removed after combat.
COMBAT_TURN_START: incoming player, from the scoped refresh primitive.
These are omniscient fixture logs, with no registered event listeners yet.

Example (run from the project root)
------------------------------------
from hearthstone.catalog import load
from hearthstone.prototype import Prototype

engine = Prototype(load())
state = engine.state()
state['entities'].append(engine.entity('axe-1', 'CS2_106', 0, 'HAND'))
state = engine.play(state, 0, 'axe-1')
state = engine.attack(state, 0, 'hero-0', 'hero-1')
# Opposing hero takes 3; axe has 1 durability; hero spent its attack.
state = engine.begin_combat_turn(state, 1)
state = engine.begin_combat_turn(state, 0)
state = engine.attack(state, 0, 'hero-0', 'hero-1')
# Opposing hero has taken 6 total; axe is broken in the graveyard.

For minion combat, play a minion, then use a later incoming combat turn to
ready it. attack_targets(state, attacker_id) enumerates currently legal
defenders. current_attack(state, entity) computes supported current Attack.
equipped_weapon(state, player) returns the equipped instance or None.

Tests and traceability
-----------------------
Run: python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
tests/test_combat.py adds 36 tests (6 catalog, 30 combat); full suite is 91.
All 55 earlier tests still run, including drawing/fatigue/burn regressions.

WPN-01/02: WeaponCatalogTests validates ID/stats, new/previous schemas,
           strict stat shapes, scripted-weapon rejection and all manifests.
WPN-03: equip, replacement, full-board equip and failed-play atomicity tests.
WPN-04: printed/instance durability isolation, current hero Attack and sheathing.
WPN-05: exhausted/frozen/cannot-attack, zero Attack, spent attacks, and
        non-character/wrong-player/zone rejection tests.
WPN-06: visible Taunt, Stealthed/Immune Taunt, invalid target and player tests.
WPN-07/08: paired minion deaths, lethal-defender retaliation, zero defender
           Attack, hero non-retaliation, Divine Shield, Armor and Spell Damage.
WPN-09: minion attack does not consume weapon; hero Immune still consumes
        durability; enemy hero defending does not consume its own weapon.
WPN-10: final durability breaks axe; replacement before/after attack;
        lethal combat/retaliation and final-state rejection.
WPN-11: refresh only incoming player's characters, preserve mana/decks/fatigue,
        sheathing and successive-turn breakage scenarios.
WPN-12: invalid attack atomicity; malformed weapon rejection; identical
        input/output, serialization and unchanged-input assertions.

Evidence and limits
---------------------

https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/cards/311-gladiators-longbow
The fixture does not implement Gladiator's Longbow; immunity is injected in
its dedicated regression scenario. No full client-log conformance is claimed.

Base combat rules follow the supplied engine specification's section 6 with
the immunity/durability correction above. This stage covers normal declarations
for the supported catalog. No combat-preparation interrupts, redirected or
cancelled attacks, reaction triggers, weapon Battlecries/Deathrattles, secrets,
forced combat, Windfury, Charge/Rush, Poisonous, Lifesteal, stat enchantment
layers or complete freeze-thaw/turn lifecycle are implemented. These require
later scheduler/keyword stages; they must not be inferred from these tests.

nested death handling, using Loot Hoarder after adding a reaction scheduler.
