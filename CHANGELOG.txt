   Provenance/limitations are also recorded in docs/reactions-stage9.txt.

   bonuses from living, non-silenced friendly SPELL_DAMAGE minions in PLAY, once
   per spell damage operation/batch. Added Kobold Geomancer CS2_142 (2 mana, 2/2,
   Spell Damage +1), bringing the catalog to 52 definitions. Verified its tags
   against HearthSim/hs-data CardDefs.xml. Tests cover stacking, Fireball,
   Flamestrike, source removal, and unaffected powers/fatigue/Battlecries.

   apply dictionaries with every legal target/position and every opening mulligan
   subset (at most 16); no simulated effects or hidden-Secret probes. Test all
   listed actions and invalid samples, and complete seeded games with invariants
   after every move. HS_RANDOM_GAMES configures the normal six-game property run.

   hide both deck orders/instances, opponent private hand and Secret identities/

   Full checkpoints/raw logs remain private. Future private random generation
